About Accessible Transit

Creating safer and more accessible public spaces through community-powered accessibility reporting.

About the Project

Accessible Transit is a web-based accessibility reporting platform designed to help people identify and report barriers in public spaces.

Users can select a location on an interactive map, describe an accessibility problem, choose its severity, and optionally upload an image as visual evidence.

The goal is to create greater awareness of accessibility barriers and support safer and more inclusive mobility for everyone.

The Problem We Address

People using wheelchairs, strollers, walking aids, or other mobility supports may face barriers such as broken ramps, blocked sidewalks, uneven roads, and inaccessible elevators.

These problems may not always be easy to identify or communicate. Accessible Transit provides a simple way for users to report such problems and share their locations with the community.

Project Objectives

Identify Barriers

Help users identify accessibility problems in public areas.

Easy Reporting

Provide a simple interface for reporting accessibility issues.

Visual Evidence

Allow users to upload images of reported accessibility barriers.

Community Awareness

Help communities become more aware of accessibility challenges.
