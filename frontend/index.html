What Accessible Transit Does

Our platform provides simple tools for identifying accessibility problems, reporting them, and creating greater awareness within the community.

Identify Barriers

Find problems such as broken ramps, blocked sidewalks, uneven roads, and inaccessible facilities.

Report Problems

Select a location on the map, describe the problem, select its severity, and submit a report.

Support Safer Mobility

Shared accessibility information can help people become more aware of barriers in public spaces.

Why Accessible Transit?

People with different mobility needs may face barriers while travelling through public spaces. Accessible Transit provides a simple way to identify, report, and share information about these problems.
